Experimental Validation of CFD Models

Catheter prototyping and Particle Image Velocimetry
CAD · 3D Printing · PIV · CFD Validation

Overview

This project focused on the experimental validation of CFD simulations of a paediatric haemodialysis catheter using Particle Image Velocimetry (PIV) measurements obtained from a dedicated flow-loop setup.

My contribution included the development and testing of different catheter prototypes for experimental measurements, as well as the adaptation of the CAD geometries for 3D printing and experimental testing.

Workflow

01 · Catheter Prototyping CAD adaptation and 3D-printed prototypes
02 · Experimental Measurements PIV measurements from a dedicated flow-loop setup
03 · CFD Comparison Comparison of experimental measurements with CFD predictions

Prototype Development

Several prototype iterations were developed using 3D printing to identify a suitable configuration for experimental testing.

The catheter geometries were adapted for manufacturing by splitting them into two interlocking parts, allowing the prototypes to be assembled without the use of adhesives.

Particle Image Velocimetry

CFD–PIV Comparison

Experimental PIV measurements were compared with CFD predictions to assess the consistency of the computational model and its ability to reproduce the experimentally observed flow behaviour.

The experimental flow-loop and PIV measurements were performed in collaboration with the University of Rostock. This project page focuses on the catheter prototyping and CFD–PIV comparison.
