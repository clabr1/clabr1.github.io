Experimental Flow Measurement

Controlled flow-loop and Particle Image Velocimetry
PIV · Flow Loop · Validation

Overview

Developed and used a controlled flow-loop with Particle Image Velocimetry to obtain experimental flow measurements for comparison with computational models.

The experimental setup was designed to provide controlled and repeatable flow conditions while enabling optical measurement of the flow field.
