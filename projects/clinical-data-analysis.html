Clinical Data Analysis

Analysis of clinical data from paediatric haemodialysis catheters
Python · Statistics · Survival Analysis · Data Visualisation

Overview

Collected and analysed clinical data of central venous catheters (CVCs) across three UK hospitals, creating a dataset covering 154 patients, 288 catheters and over 85,000 catheter-days.

The work contributed to the assessment of the clinical performance of paediatric CVCs with different design and size. Relevant results included: 54.2% of catheters experienced dysfunction, 43.9% required removal and 25% patients had 3+ catheters

Published work
Clinical performance and microstructural assessment of haemodialysis catheters for children on maintenance dialysis.

What I did

Data cleaning and preparation; statistical testing and survival analysis to compare different catheters models; visualisation of the results. I performed all analyses in Python using Jupyter Notebook.
