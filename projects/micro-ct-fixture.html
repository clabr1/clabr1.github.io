Micro-CT Fixture Design

CAD, 3D printing and micro-CT positioning
CAD · 3D Printing · Micro-CT

Overview

Designed and fabricated a custom fixture to position haemodialysis catheters for micro-CT imaging, from CAD design through 3D printing and scanner setup.

The fixture was developed to provide stable and repeatable positioning of the catheter during imaging while maintaining access to the relevant regions of interest.
