Catheter Design & DOE

From micro-CT geometry to CAD, CFD setup and Design of Experiments
CAD · CFD · DOE · Micro-CT

Overview

Developed a workflow to translate catheter geometry obtained from micro-CT imaging into a computational modelling framework.

The workflow combined geometric measurements, CAD reconstruction, CFD setup and Design of Experiments to investigate the influence of catheter geometry.

Workflow

01 · Micro-CT Imaging and geometric assessment
02 · CAD Geometry reconstruction in SolidWorks
03 · CFD Computational model and solver setup
04 · DOE Systematic study of design parameters

Micro-CT & Geometry

CAD Design

The measured geometry was translated into CAD models to create controlled and reproducible computational geometries.

CFD Setup

The CAD geometries were prepared for CFD analysis, including computational domain preparation, meshing and solver setup.

Design of Experiments

A Design of Experiments framework was developed to systematically investigate the effect of selected geometric parameters.

The project page focuses on the modelling workflow and methodology; detailed optimisation results are not shown.
